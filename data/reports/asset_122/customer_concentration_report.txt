### Company Performance Update

As part of our ongoing monitoring, we have identified a key area that warrants attention regarding the company's financial stability.

#### Revenue Concentration Risk

The evaluation of risks associated with the dependence on a limited number of customers has shown concerning results. This situation can potentially compromise the bargaining power and stability of revenue. Our analysis suggests that there is an increased vulnerability in this aspect, which could have long-term implications for the company's success.

#### Recommendations

To mitigate this risk, we recommend immediate action to diversify customer base and reduce dependence on a few major customers. Strategies may include targeted marketing efforts, exploration of new business opportunities, and strengthening relationships with current clients to encourage broader support.
