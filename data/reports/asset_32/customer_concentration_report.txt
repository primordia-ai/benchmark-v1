**Company Performance Review**

We are taking a closer look at one of the key areas that could impact our company's long-term success. Our analysis suggests that there may be some concerns regarding our revenue streams.

Our research indicates that we have become overly reliant on a small number of customers, which can lead to an imbalance in bargaining power and create instability in our revenue. This is not a desirable situation, as it leaves us vulnerable to potential losses if these key customers were to reduce their business with us or leave altogether.

While we have made some efforts to diversify our customer base, it appears that more work needs to be done in this area. We must take steps to mitigate the risks associated with an over-reliance on a few large customers and focus on developing relationships with a broader range of clients.

This is a critical issue that requires immediate attention from management. We recommend exploring strategies to reduce our dependence on these key customers, such as investing in marketing efforts to attract new business or implementing measures to enhance customer retention. By doing so, we can create a more stable and sustainable revenue model for the company.
