**Company Financial Performance Report**
======================================

Our analysis of the company's financial performance reveals some areas of concern that need to be addressed.

The **Dependence on Key Customers** remains a significant issue for the company. While efforts have been made to diversify revenue streams, the current level of dependence on a select group of customers is unsustainable in the long term. This concentration of risk poses a threat to the company's bargaining power and revenue stability.

We are seeing **red flags** in this area, indicating that immediate attention is required to mitigate these risks. To address this challenge, we recommend accelerating diversification efforts and developing strategies to reduce reliance on key customers.

By taking proactive steps to reduce dependence on a small number of customers, the company can improve its financial resilience and create a more stable revenue stream. We will continue to monitor this area closely and provide updates as new information becomes available.
