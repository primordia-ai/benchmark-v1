**Performance Review**

The company's financial performance has shown significant improvement in terms of managing its customer base. Specifically, the review highlights a positive trend in the diversification of revenue streams.

This positive development can be expected to lead to increased bargaining power for the company and greater stability in revenue streams.

Overall, this trend is viewed as a significant accomplishment, indicating a more balanced and sustainable approach to managing revenue generation.
