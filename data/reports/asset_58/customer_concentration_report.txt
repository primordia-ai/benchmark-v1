**Business Report**

**Executive Summary**
Our analysis of the company's financial performance reveals concerns regarding its exposure to risks associated with customer dependence. This vulnerability can compromise the company's bargaining power and revenue stability.

**Key Findings**

* The company's reliance on a limited number of customers has led to an unfavorable concentration of revenue.
* Efforts to diversify and mitigate this risk have been inadequate, leaving the company vulnerable to changes in the market or customer behavior.

**Implications**
The current situation poses significant risks to the company's financial stability. A sudden loss of one of its key customers could have a substantial impact on revenue, potentially leading to reduced profitability and decreased investor confidence.

**Recommendations**

* The company should prioritize efforts to diversify its customer base and reduce dependence on a limited number of clients.
* Management should implement strategies to enhance bargaining power and revenue stability, such as negotiating longer-term contracts or expanding into new markets.

By addressing these concerns and implementing measures to mitigate the risks associated with customer dependence, the company can improve its financial resilience and position itself for long-term success.
