**Performance Update**

Our analysis suggests that the company's ability to mitigate potential risks associated with leadership transitions remains a concern. The current state of succession planning for key roles indicates a lack of clear continuity in case of unexpected departures.

Management's attention is drawn to this area, as it has the potential to impact overall performance and competitiveness in the long term.

Recommendations will be made to address these concerns in future updates.
