**Financial Performance Review**
====================================

As we continue to monitor the financial health of our company, we are taking a closer look at certain soft indicators that provide insight into its overall performance.

One area of concern is the dependence on a limited number of customers for revenue. While this may not be immediately apparent, it can have significant implications for bargaining power and revenue stability in the long run.

Our analysis suggests that there is an elevated risk associated with this issue. Specifically, the company's reliance on a small group of customers poses a threat to its financial sustainability.

To address this concern, we recommend a focus on diversification efforts to reduce dependence on these key clients. By expanding our customer base and developing more robust relationships with multiple stakeholders, we can mitigate this risk and create a more stable revenue stream.

Action Items:

* Conduct an in-depth review of the top customers contributing to revenue
* Identify opportunities for growth and expansion into new markets or customer segments
* Develop strategies to foster stronger relationships with existing clients and attract new ones

By prioritizing diversification and addressing this underlying issue, we can strengthen our company's financial position and create a more resilient business model.
