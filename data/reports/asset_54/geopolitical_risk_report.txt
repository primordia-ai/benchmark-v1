While this is an area of continued focus for us, it's reassuring to note that our efforts have paid off, and we're able to navigate these challenges with minimal disruption.

Overall, our position in this regard remains neutral, and we'll continue to closely monitor the situation to ensure we're prepared for any potential changes.
