**Company Performance Review**

Our analysis has revealed areas of concern in the company's financial health. Specifically, we have identified a significant vulnerability that warrants attention from management.

One key metric that stands out as particularly problematic is the concentration of revenue among a small number of customers. This trend poses a substantial risk to the company's bargaining power and revenue stability.

In this regard, our analysis suggests that the company has not made sufficient progress in diversifying its customer base. While efforts have been undertaken to mitigate this issue, it appears that more work is needed to alleviate the risks associated with over-reliance on a small number of customers.

Management should prioritize addressing this weakness to avoid potential disruptions to revenue streams and minimize exposure to market fluctuations.
