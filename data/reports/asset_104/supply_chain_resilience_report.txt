**Quarterly Review**

The company's ability to withstand external shocks to its supply chain has been a concern in the current quarter. Our analysis suggests that the company may not be adequately prepared for potential disruptions.

While the company's supplier base is not highly diversified, it does have some mechanisms in place to manage inventory levels and mitigate risks. However, these measures may not be sufficient to withstand significant external shocks.

The review also highlighted a lack of investment in technology integration, which could exacerbate the issue if it were to encounter a major supply chain disruption. This is an area that management should prioritize in order to enhance the company's resilience.

The overall assessment suggests that there are risks associated with the company's current supply chain configuration. We will continue to monitor this situation and provide updates as necessary.
