### Business Performance Review

As part of our ongoing analysis, we have identified areas that require attention to ensure the company's continued growth and stability.

One area of concern revolves around the revenue model. Our assessments indicate that there is a high dependence on a small group of customers. This not only affects bargaining power but also poses a risk to revenue stability. Efforts aimed at diversifying revenue streams are crucial at this stage.

Our findings suggest that there is room for improvement in this area, which, if addressed appropriately, could lead to more sustainable and stable growth in the long term. The management's approach towards mitigating these risks will be closely monitored as we continue to review the company's performance.
