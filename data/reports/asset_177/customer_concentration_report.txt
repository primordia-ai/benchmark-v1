### Company Performance Review

The evaluation of the company's dependence on a limited set of customers has shown concerning results. This aspect significantly impacts the company's bargaining power and affects revenue stability.

#### Key Findings:

- **Dependence on Top Customers:** The data indicates that there is an overreliance on a small number of customers, which is a critical concern for future business sustainability.
- **Diversification Efforts:** Although efforts to diversify the customer base have been noted, their effectiveness in significantly reducing dependence on these key customers has not been demonstrated.

#### Analysis:

This issue raises questions about the long-term viability of the current revenue model. The ability to negotiate favorable terms with a limited number of major clients is compromised when the company's success is heavily dependent on them. Furthermore, the lack of diversity in the customer base makes it vulnerable to market fluctuations and potential loss of any single significant client.

#### Recommendations:

- **Immediate Diversification Efforts:** Enhance and expedite efforts to attract and retain a broader customer base, thereby reducing reliance on a few major clients.
- **Revenue Model Review:** Conduct an internal review to assess the revenue model's resilience against market changes and the impact of losing any key client.

By addressing these concerns proactively, the company can mitigate risks associated with overdependence on a limited number of customers and improve its overall financial stability.
