**Business Report**

The company's financial performance has been scrutinized, revealing concerns regarding its exposure to risk. Specifically, the evaluation of one key aspect of the business indicates a negative trend.

A critical metric, representing the potential vulnerability to market fluctuations and customer concentration, suggests that the company is overly reliant on a small number of customers. This raises significant concerns about the bargaining power of these customers and the stability of revenue streams.

As a result, management must reassess strategies aimed at mitigating this risk, including diversification efforts. Focusing on expanding customer bases and developing new revenue streams will be essential in reducing dependence on key clients and fostering a more stable financial landscape.

The company's leadership is urged to prioritize initiatives that promote increased customer diversity and reduce the concentration of revenue sources. This proactive approach will enable the business to better withstand market fluctuations and ensure long-term sustainability.
