### Company Performance Review

We are pleased to report that our assessment of the company's exposure to external risks has yielded a positive outcome.

In evaluating the potential impact of international political tensions, trade disputes, sanctions, and instability in key operational regions on our business operations, we have found that our company is well-positioned to navigate these challenges. The currency fluctuations and disruptions to supply chain operations commonly associated with such risks do not appear to pose significant threats to our operations.

This positive assessment reflects a proactive approach to risk management and the implementation of effective strategies to mitigate potential vulnerabilities. Our ability to adapt to an ever-changing global economic landscape has been demonstrated through this indicator, showcasing resilience and agility in response to external pressures.

The company's performance in managing these external risks is indicative of a robust foundation for future growth and success, underscoring the importance of strategic planning and risk management in achieving long-term business objectives.
