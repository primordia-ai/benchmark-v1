**Company Performance Review**
=====================================

As part of our ongoing evaluation, we have been monitoring certain key performance indicators that provide insights into the company's operational health.

One such metric has raised some concerns regarding the company's revenue model. Specifically, we have observed a trend that suggests an over-reliance on a limited number of customers. This concentration of revenue risk is not ideal and may ultimately impact the company's bargaining power and revenue stability.

While efforts to diversify revenue streams are being made, more work is needed to mitigate this vulnerability. Management will need to prioritize initiatives aimed at reducing dependence on a small group of clients and building stronger relationships with a broader base of customers.

**Recommendations**
--------------------

*   Continue to monitor the concentration of revenue risk
*   Develop strategies to diversify revenue streams
*   Prioritize efforts to strengthen relationships with multiple customers

**Next Steps**
--------------

We will continue to track this metric and provide regular updates on any changes or improvements in this area.
