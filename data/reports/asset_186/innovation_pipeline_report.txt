Our analysis of the company's performance reveals some concerns regarding its capacity for ongoing innovation.

While there are signs that the company is taking steps to address these issues, our overall assessment remains cautious. We will continue to monitor the situation and provide updates as more information becomes available.

* Lack of cross-functional collaboration across departments
* Disappointing product rollouts with limited market impact
