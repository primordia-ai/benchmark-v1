**Business Report**

The company's performance in maintaining a competitive advantage has shown signs of weakness. Despite efforts to establish unique products and intellectual property, the market has failed to respond positively. Exclusive partnerships that were previously viewed as key assets have not yielded the expected returns.

While investments in research and development continue to drive growth, the inability to create sustainable barriers to entry for competitors is a significant concern. The company's inability to translate its innovations into long-term competitive advantages raises questions about its ability to sustain its market share.

Management will need to reassess their strategies to address these challenges and consider alternative approaches to maintaining a competitive edge in the market.

**Recommendations**

* Re-evaluate exclusive partnerships to ensure they are providing adequate returns
* Increase investment in research and development to drive innovation
* Explore new ways to create sustainable barriers to entry for competitors

These actions will help the company to address its current weaknesses and position itself for future growth.
