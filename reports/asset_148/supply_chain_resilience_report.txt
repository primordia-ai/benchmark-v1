**Company's Vulnerability to Disruptions Remains a Concern**

Our analysis of the company's supply chain resilience reveals ongoing challenges in adapting to disruptions. Despite efforts to improve, vulnerabilities persist.

The company's lack of diversification among suppliers has left it exposed to potential supplier failures or geopolitical events. Inventory management strategies have also been criticized for being inadequate, leading to stockouts and lost sales opportunities.

Furthermore, the limited integration of technology into its supply chain operations has hindered the company's ability to respond quickly to disruptions. While some progress has been made in this area, more work is needed to bridge the gap with industry peers.

The ongoing risk of disruption-related losses remains a pressing concern for investors and stakeholders. Until the company addresses these underlying issues, it will struggle to achieve its full potential.

We recommend that management prioritize efforts to strengthen supply chain resilience through increased diversification, improved inventory management, and enhanced technology integration. By doing so, the company can better withstand disruptions and capitalize on opportunities in an increasingly complex business environment.
