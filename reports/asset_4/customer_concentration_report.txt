**Company Performance Review**

We are pleased to report that our analysis has revealed a positive trend in the company's dependence on key customers. This aspect of the business has shown significant improvement, indicating a more stable and diversified revenue stream.

The management's focus on reducing reliance on a small number of major clients has paid off, with efforts yielding favorable results. The proportion of revenue generated from these critical customers is now within healthy limits, allowing for better bargaining power and reduced vulnerability to potential customer losses.

This development is a testament to the company's ability to adapt and mitigate risks associated with over-reliance on a few key customers. As a result, we anticipate improved revenue stability and increased flexibility in navigating market fluctuations.
