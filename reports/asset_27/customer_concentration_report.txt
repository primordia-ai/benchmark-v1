**Company Performance Report**

The financial performance of the company has shown a positive trend in managing its revenue dependence on a small number of customers. 

Management's efforts to reduce this dependence have paid off, with notable improvements in the stability of revenue streams.

While still dependent on a select few large clients, the overall impact of potential risks associated with excessive reliance has decreased significantly, making the company more resilient to changes in market conditions and customer dynamics.

The increased diversification of revenue sources is expected to further reduce this risk over time. 

In light of these developments, we believe that the company's management team has effectively mitigated one of its key vulnerabilities.

Recommendations for investors remain cautiously optimistic.
