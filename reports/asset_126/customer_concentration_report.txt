**Performance Review**
=======================

Our analysis of the company's financial performance reveals some areas of concern regarding its dependence on a select group of customers.

One key metric indicates that the company's revenue is heavily skewed towards a small number of clients. This concentration of risk could potentially limit the company's bargaining power and make it vulnerable to fluctuations in revenue.

While efforts are being made to diversify the customer base, the current state of affairs suggests that the company is not yet adequately insulated against potential risks associated with over-reliance on these key customers.

Our assessment recommends a review of the company's sales strategy and a more focused effort to attract new business from a broader range of clients. This will help to mitigate the risks associated with an overly concentrated revenue stream.

In terms of specific actions, we suggest:

* A thorough review of the company's customer base and identification of opportunities for diversification
* Development of targeted strategies to attract new customers and expand existing relationships
* Implementation of metrics to track progress towards a more diversified revenue stream

By taking proactive steps to address this issue, the company can reduce its exposure to risk and improve its overall financial stability.
