**Company Performance Update**

As part of our ongoing analysis, we have been monitoring the company's reliance on a limited number of key customers. This aspect has raised some concerns regarding the potential risks associated with such dependence.

The data indicates that the company still heavily relies on a small group of customers for its revenue. While this can provide stability in terms of cash flow, it also creates an imbalance in bargaining power and exposes the company to significant risk if any one of these key customers were to leave or reduce their business with the company.

Management will need to prioritize diversification efforts to mitigate this risk and ensure long-term financial stability.

**Recommendations**

* Develop a comprehensive plan to expand customer base and reduce dependence on key accounts
* Implement strategies to enhance revenue streams from multiple sources
* Monitor and review progress regularly to adjust course as needed

This is an ongoing process, and we will continue to monitor the company's performance in this area.
